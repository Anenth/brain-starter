# Chase ledger

What is owed, both ways. One line each. The morning brief surfaces anything older than
5 days. The check-in skill and inbox triage keep this current.

Format: `- [ ] **who**: what · asked YYYY-MM-DD · due YYYY-MM-DD · wikilink to the company or meeting`
Optional: `parked till YYYY-MM-DD` when the owner is on leave. `bin/brain chases` reads
asked, due and parked from the line. Dates are in the owner's timezone (brain.json).
When it lands: strike it with the date. Never delete.
