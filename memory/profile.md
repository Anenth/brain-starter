## How to write for me

<!-- The house style. Every brief, card and draft follows it. Change it to suit you.
     bin/brain lint and health count em dashes, so keep that rule or edit the lint. -->

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Plain words. No jargon.
- Small paragraphs. White space between them.
- Bullets for any list or set of steps.
- Bold only the few words that matter most.
- No italics.
- No em dashes. Use a full stop and a new sentence instead.
- Say it straight. No warm-up lines, no filler, no padding.
- Long reply? Put a 1-2 line summary at the top.
- Offering a choice? Give the pick first, then the reason.
